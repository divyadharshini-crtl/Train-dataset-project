Midnight Rollover Duration Engine

Trains frequently cross midnight on multi-day journeys. Built an elegant, vectorized cumulative duration algorithm (Level 2) to calculate travel times.

  • Travel Leg: Departure (i-1) to Arrival (i). If Arrival < Departure, adds 24 hours to represent midnight crossover.
  • Halt Leg: Arrival (i) to Departure (i). If Departure < Arrival, adds 24 hours to represent station halts crossing midnight.
  • High Performance: Processes 186k rows in under 0.3 seconds.
